FIELD MANUAL / QUESTIONS

Know the rules.
Choose your own way.

What is a story, operation or mission?

A story is a complete 24-hour escape. It contains five consecutive operations, each a route with two mandatory collection missions. There are 50 stories, 250 operation routes and 500 required missions, plus two optional resource contacts per route. They share nine geographic regions; they are not 250 separate map downloads.

Does the clock reset between operations?

No. Time, cash, energy and inventory carry across all five. A different story starts a new 24-hour run. Later operations progressively increase journey time, energy effort and mission duration.

Why are there two timers?

Time to reach safety is the deadline for your entire escape or practice challenge. Border closes in is a separate crossing window on scenarios that have one. Finish a crossing before its earlier limit. “Open” means no scheduled crossing closure; delays and other requirements still apply.

Why can’t I go straight to the goal?

Each story operation requires its two mission items in order. Practice scenarios also require an intermediate stop. Complete the pickups before you reach the final operation destination.

Why can’t I select Flight?

Flights only connect the two designated air terminals in a world expedition. In Japan these are Osaka Itami (ITM) and Tokyo Haneda (HND), with proper landside ground access. Reach the departure terminal first and select the destination terminal. Flight is unavailable in the detailed Brest corridor and between ordinary map points.

Can I walk anywhere?

Walking is available where the map’s game network connects. In Brest and Japan that means mapped paths or roads, with water and mapped access restrictions respected. Other world hubs allow schematic ground travel within the same city; you cannot walk between distant hubs or over the sea.

Will a failed departure take my money?

No fare is charged for a rejected leg. Planning seconds continue while the clock is running. Earlier successful legs and completed actions still count. Clicking Go repeatedly cannot settle the same journey twice.

How do optional resources work?

Open Optional cash & recovery, travel to a contact, then press Collect. Each is usable once per operation. Collection costs the displayed time. Energy is capped at 100%; a full-energy collection is rejected without charge. Optional contacts never replace the two required missions.

Why did the game force me to rest?

Walking at low energy triggers 25-minute recovery stops. The transport quote includes them. The animation pauses movement while energy rises. The full fare and simulated time are settled once on arrival.

Are borders, flights and events live?

No. All services, prices, restrictions and story events are deterministic game fiction. The core works offline and uses no live feeds. Satellite imagery is archival. Do not use the game to plan real travel.

Does the whole world have detailed roads?

All playable mission areas do: Brest plus 16 cities and their airport corridors use offline OSM road packs. This is bounded coverage around the game routes, rather than a download of every road on Earth. Airport markers use real landside terminal locations.

Are my saves stored online?

No. Saves and completion records remain in this browser on this device. Each story can be resumed from Stories. On a regional map upgrade, old untravelled schematic plans are cleared and the game pauses; cash, energy, time, completed missions and items remain. Clearing browser site storage removes them. The original eight-section Nightfall escape remains available for old saves.

Can I pause, replay or skip the introduction?

Yes. Pause freezes planning and blocks actions; it does not undo a journey. Stories lets you resume or start over. The five-slide first-time introduction can be skipped and replayed with ?. Reduce motion is in Settings.

Does it cost anything? Can I use it offline?

No accounts, paid services, API keys or runtime AI calls are required. After the first complete load, the service worker caches the core game, globe, Brest map, help and FAQ. Each world story’s two regional packs are downloaded and cached when you first open that corridor. Open that region once online before expecting it to work offline. Optional NASA satellite imagery requires an internet connection.

Why does the browser show an older version?

Open Settings → Refresh to latest version or use the update recovery page. It refreshes this game’s cached files and keeps local saves.

Where can I try the new intelligence, timetables and document puzzles?

Open Stories → The Shelter Keys, marked Field-desk pilot. This one five-operation story has source-stamped reports, dispatch verification, scheduled shuttles and flights, and a two-field document check per operation. Open Field desk or Ask HQ. The other stories keep their existing rules. The pilot works offline after downloading Japan’s packs. See the field-desk guide.

Can I back up my game or undo a restart?

Yes. Open Missions → Help & settings → Settings → Save backup & recovery. Download a JSON file, or choose one to review before confirming restoration. One local recovery copy is kept before restart/restore, and can be recovered from the same screen. Restored games stay paused. Files contain one run, including documents and pending journeys; maps, settings and completion records are not included. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing site storage removes the recovery copy, so keep a downloaded backup.

Where can I try pursuit and hiding?

Stories → New pursuit pilot → The watched handoff. Contact handoffs and shuttle tickets leave traces. HQ reports search areas and warning times; the pursuer follows the offline roads between reports. ◈ marks three one-use hiding places. Hiding spends quoted time and money to wait out a known local search; Pause freezes the clock. The original courier challenge and the other 50 stories keep their existing rules. See the pursuit guide.

Is there a complete five-operation pursuit story?

Yes: Stories → The watched route · Full five-operation story. It runs from Osaka to Tokyo over one shared 24 hours, with ten mission actions, optional cash/energy, pursuit, shelter access, police inspections, an airport document puzzle, a scheduled Itami–Haneda flight and an HQ delivery redirect. The last approach is on foot. Existing stories and pilots keep their original rules. See the five-operation guide.

Why is my game paused when I return?

Opening or reloading the game never starts the clock automatically. Press Resume game when ready. An unfinished journey waits too, then settles its time and fare once.

How do I know I have the latest version?

The loaded version appears beside the brand. When an update is ready, choose Update game; progress is saved and the new version opens paused. If an older cached release is stuck, use Settings → Refresh to latest version while online.
