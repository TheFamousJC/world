JOURNEY COMPLETE

You made it.

A real map. A fictional journey.

Detailed map coverage: Brest–Terespol–Biała Podlaska plus all 16 worldwide mission cities and their airport corridors. Roads, railways, waterways, buildings and airport geometry come from OpenStreetMap; the surrounding globe uses Natural Earth reference geography. Each story downloads its two bounded city packs for offline play.

All scenarios, pickup points, transport services, opening windows, prices and travel documents are game fiction. Airport markers use actual landside terminal areas; no live flight schedule is supplied. Shelter and supply locations are invented. This game does not establish real-world access, services or safety.

Brest OSM snapshot: 24 July 2026. Westward extension retrieved from OpenStreetMap on 20 October 2026. Worldwide road packs retrieved 21 October 2026 from bounded Overpass extracts. Building heights are approximate where the source has no height.

© OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL · Full licences and attribution

MapLibre GL JS 6.15.0 · Open Sans · Natural Earth. The vector map works offline. Optional satellite imagery: NASA GIBS / USGS / SDSU, Landsat WELD annual mosaic, archival 2000 (30 m). Satellite mode needs internet and does not show current conditions.
