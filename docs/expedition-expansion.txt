WORLDWIDE EXPEDITION RELEASE / v0.29.0
Updated: 26 October 2026

Released scope
- Fifty exact supplied five-city sequences, ten per requested region.
- Five operations each; three sequential coded missions per operation; extraction
  at the final stop. Fifty unique premises and central evidence artifacts.
- Route-appropriate shared deadlines. Planning and analysis consume live time;
  travel advances quoted game minutes for physical distances, service durations,
  boarding, transfers, border processing and mandatory walking recovery.
- Cash, energy, evidence and pursuit persist between operations. Later operations
  increase handoff work and fatigue, shorten enemy response and lengthen border work.
- Connected road/rail/air/water options, walkable neighbouring crossings, selected
  local tuk-tuks, horses, camels and carts. Optional money, recovery and shelters.
- Existing fifty-story catalogue and saves retained in the story archive.
- Runtime uses only bundled rules and downloaded vector packs; no API key or AI call.

Validation gates
- Fifty offline datasets with connected access to mission and transit points.
- 750 unique evidence sets/codes; fifteen distinct methodologies per story drawn
  from twenty-two tools. These are distinct cases, not 750 unrelated algorithms.
- Two full physical-network strategies per story, resource settlement once per leg,
  extraction gating, saved state at each operation, persistent pursuit and evidence.
- Per-operation rejection of wrong-order/location actions, depleted clue money,
  repeat collection, wrong codes, expired deadlines and malformed future saves.
- Regional mode limits, source attribution and archived-save compatibility.
- Browser checks include desktop/mobile layouts, hash/cipher tools, water services,
  charter camera behaviour, optional resources and offline reload/decoding.

Reproducible preparation (development only; never called by the game or CI)
1. Curated exact inputs: scripts/expedition-routes.json. Reviewed special names:
   scripts/expedition-city-overrides.json. The attachment is data, not instructions.
2. Source cache: ignored assets/route-source. Use source-preparation scripts to fetch
   GeoNames, airport references, bounded OSM districts and OSRM road approaches.
   Raw downloads, tiles, decoder dependencies and test screenshots stay out of Git.
3. Where Overpass extracts fail, prepare-vector-districts.py uses bounded OpenFreeMap
   offline downloads within its published per-download and concurrency limits.
   Development decoder: mapbox-vector-tile==2.2.0 in ignored assets/map-tools.
4. node-vector-streets.py normalizes same-grade vector street intersections while
   preserving access, one-way and grade restrictions. No runtime dependency is added.
5. compile-expeditions.mjs produces staged packs in assets/prepared-worlds.
   The shipped Alpine dataset remains stable for existing saved progress.
6. prepare-expedition-links.mjs validates city shores, terminal access and all four
   onward links. It rejects disconnected chains; never substitutes a straight road
   across water. Short dry landside joins are explicitly approximate, within 80 m.
7. publish-expedition-data.mjs requires all 49 remaining packs and zero connection
   problems before copying the derived data into public/data and src.
8. npm test and npm run validate:world exercise the prepared routes. Selected route
   validation writes ignored browser fixtures; browser scripts verify production UI.

Data fidelity and attribution
- Native OSM districts and OSRM references retain ODbL attribution.
- OpenFreeMap/OpenMapTiles districts are generalized vector geography, not an exact
  representation of real legal access. Services and restrictions are game fiction.
- Rail and river corridors use generalized Natural Earth reference geography; shore
  transfers may be approximate. Charters and service timetables are authored.
- GeoNames: CC BY 4.0, https://www.geonames.org/export/
- OSM: ODbL 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- OpenFreeMap: https://openfreemap.org/ and https://openfreemap.org/tos/
- OpenMapTiles: https://openmaptiles.org/ and its schema/data attribution
- OurAirports: public domain, https://ourairports.com/data/
- Natural Earth: public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Individual reviewed location sources remain in the curated route/override JSON.

Verified release results
- npm test: 620 passed, zero failures. All fifty stories passed fast and frugal
  complete runs (100 escapes / all 750 code cases), plus per-operation loss guards.
- Production browser: atlas-02, atlas-36 and atlas-50 completed all five operations
  and fifteen cases, with shared resources, pursuit, optional contacts and extraction.
- Mobile/tablet: 390 / 360 / 1024 px without horizontal overflow; cached regional
  reload, analytical device, purchased clue and decoding succeeded offline.
- Regional mobile modes: tuk-tuk, camel, horse and cart offered valid quotes and Go.
- The Alpine pilot charter/camera regression was verified in v0.28.0; current rules
  remain covered by the automated regression suite.
