EXPEDITION EXPANSION: FIRST FIVE-CITY STORY / v0.28.0
Updated: 26 October 2026

Accepted design
- Preserve the exact 50 supplied city sequences, including the original country labels.
- Five operations: prepare in city 1, then travel through cities 2, 3, 4 and 5.
- Three sequential code missions per operation; the last city is extraction.
- One shared route-appropriate deadline, cash balance, energy and pursuer state.
- Planning and analysis consume live time. Travel advances quoted game minutes from
  mapped distances, transport, service duration, waits, transfers and border delays.
  Animation length must never change the charged journey duration. Pausing freezes it.
- Regional modes must use suitable terminals and physical connections. No invented
  driving or walking line over water. All contacts and service availability are fiction.

First playable integration
- Stories: NEW - The Alpine Ledger enables atlas-01 only. Five operations follow
  Munich, Innsbruck, Brenner Pass, Bolzano and Verona, ending at extraction.
- One distance/service-based 34-hour deadline, intercity coaches on 90-minute departure
  windows, real airport approaches and generalized rail options. Fifteen different
  analytical methods, paid clues, safe addresses and carried pursuit/resources.
- The former fifty-story catalogue and existing saves remain available.
- Tests cover two full strategies, sequential packets, terminal access, missed departures,
  saves, extraction, pursuit losses/hiding, desktop/mobile and offline browser play.

Verified foundation
- scripts/expedition-routes.json: all 50 exact five-stop sequences, with attributed
  coordinate references. Ten stories per requested region.
- src/expedition-narratives.js: 50 distinct authored premises and evidence artifacts.
- src/expedition-cases.js: 750 distinct evidence sets and four-digit answers.
  Each story has 15 different methodologies, drawn from 22 offline analytical tools.
  This means 750 different cases, not 750 unrelated cryptographic algorithms.
- src/expedition-tools.js: local analytical devices, including SHA-256 via Web Crypto.
- src/expedition-clock.js: pure distance/service-based travel and shared-deadline rules.
- Tests exercise every case, independent reference vectors, timing and known ambiguous
  place names. The current published game and its saves are not migrated by this work.

Not complete / release gate
- The other 49 replacement stories are not enabled. Only atlas-01 is offered as a featured pilot.
- 30 map datasets were prepared locally; 20 district downloads could not be obtained.
  Existing source data for Khasan, Mohan and Bonfim was then found to resolve to the
  wrong homonymous locations. Their curated coordinates are corrected, and affected
  cached route responses have been quarantined for replacement.
- Map source requests returned repeated HTTP 502 errors, HTTP 406 for read-only GET
  checks, or read timeouts. Private.coffee and a second documented provider also timed
  out. Download loops were stopped; no paid or runtime map dependency was substituted.
- Local, unfinished integration is retained under ignored assets/expedition-wip.
  It is outside the release. Some trial strategies still fail on energy or pursuit.
- Complete a geographic audit, all 50 offline maps, suitable civilian airport references,
  actual ferry/river connections, terminal access and animal-mode limits.
- Extend the working pilot integration to reviewed routes only. Complete boat/ferry,
  animal-mode availability, other geographic briefings and transport/pursuit validation.
- For every story verify two full five-operation strategies, resource accounting,
  sequential mission reveal, extraction gating, several losing cases, save/reload and
  pursuit carry-over. Then perform desktop/mobile/offline browser checks and publish.

Source preparation (development only; never called by the game or CI)
- Raw downloads remain in ignored assets/route-source, never in Git.
- prepare-expedition-cities.py accepts --source with the supplied route text, treating
  its contents as data. It requires GeoNames cities500.txt and countryInfo.txt locally.
- Primary place names outrank alternate names; reviewed overrides resolve ambiguity.
  Coordinates still require geographic review before map preparation is considered done.
- fetch-expedition-sources.py reads the committed curated route list and an airport-codes
  CSV in the ignored cache, enriches airfields and requests bounded districts and routes.
  Air bases, highway strips and explicitly military names are excluded from new selection;
  remaining airfield choices still need civilian suitability review.
- fetch-expedition-airports.py prepares routed approaches from that enriched output.
- retry-expedition-districts.py supports smaller per-city requests. Do not run repeated
  loops during provider outages. Keep throttling and inspect every missing connection.
- build-expedition-puzzles.mjs regenerates the 750 cases and validation inputs locally.
  Node alone runs the puzzle tests. No API key, network call or AI service is needed.
